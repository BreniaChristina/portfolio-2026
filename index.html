02 / Web design

Digital spaces built with intention.

SEO and Local SearchMy Burg HealthVisit live site ↗

My Burg Health is a custom website created for a local chiropractic practice, designed to attract new patients while providing a fast, intuitive user experience. Built on the WordPress content management system, the site features a custom front-end developed with hand-coded HTML and CSS using the UIkit framework to deliver a clean, responsive design.

In addition to the design and development, I implemented a comprehensive local SEO strategy, including optimized site architecture, metadata, keyword targeting, and technical SEO best practices to improve the practice's visibility in local search results. The result is a modern, high-performing website that not only reflects the clinic's brand but is built to increase online visibility, generate qualified leads, and support long-term business growth.

DesignMs. Rosie's Little AchieversVisit live site ↗

Ms. Rosie's Little Achievers is a custom website created for an early childhood learning center, designed to give parents a warm, welcoming introduction to the school while making essential information easy to find. The site organizes programs by age group and guides families toward key actions such as exploring enrollment options, requesting a tour, and contacting the center.

Built on WordPress with a custom front-end using HTML and CSS, the website balances a playful, family-friendly visual identity with clear navigation and responsive design. Content throughout the site highlights Ms. Rosie's research-based curriculum, parent communication, and distinctive "grandma love" approach to childcare, creating a digital experience that reflects the personal, nurturing atmosphere of the center while supporting enrollment and local visibility.

Just For FunTimed QuizVisit live site ↗

I challenged myself to build this retro arcade-inspired timed quiz from the ground up using only vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. Every interaction, animation, and game mechanic was custom-coded, with a focus on creating an engaging, nostalgic experience that feels like a classic arcade game. This project gave me the opportunity to sharpen my front-end development skills while exploring creative UI design, game logic, and user experience in a playful, interactive format.

03 / Video

Stories with room to breathe.

Brand Film

Bhujang Style's Orphic Tank

Created to launch Bhujang Style's Orphic Tank, this promotional video blends cinematic visuals with athletic performance to showcase the brand's latest apparel. The piece features Derrick Townsel, a former professional wide receiver who signed with the Jacksonville Jaguars as an undrafted free agent in 2010 before transitioning into a career as a yogi, perfectly reflecting the brand's focus on strength, movement, and mindfulness.

Filmed on location in downtown St. Petersburg, Florida, the video was created for social media and published across Bhujang Style's and Yoga for Men's Instagram and Facebook channels. I was responsible for the entire production process—including filming, editing, and motion graphics—crafting a polished, high-impact promotional piece designed to capture attention, reinforce the brand's identity, and generate excitement for the product launch.

Passion Project

Made for the Love of Creating

Sometimes the best creative projects have no client, no deadline, and no agenda. This music video was created simply to showcase my best friend's music and to experiment with visual storytelling. Without a script or storyboard, we embraced a spontaneous, improvisational approach, letting the music and environment shape each scene organically.

I filmed the project using my Canon C100 Mark II and edited the final piece in Adobe Premiere Pro. To reinforce the mood, I intentionally added an interlaced, out of focused, lo-fi aesthetic—the subtle horizontal scan lines and vintage texture evoke the look of aging analog video, giving the project a nostalgic, dreamlike quality. Beyond the visuals, I also contributed the background vocals featured in the track, making this a truly collaborative creative project from both a visual and musical perspective.

This piece reflects my passion for cinematography, editing, music, and creating compelling work simply for the love of the creative process.
