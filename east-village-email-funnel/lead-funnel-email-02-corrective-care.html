Today, our healthcare system offers many procedures to relieve pain. These procedures generally fit into one of two categories:

Relief Care or Corrective Care

Relief Care
✕ Usually includes pharmaceuticals
✕ Provides temporary relief
✕ Does not fix the source
✕ May mask symptoms while tissue damage remains
  
Corrective Care
✓ Addresses the root cause
✓ Heals the source of pain
✓ Encourages damaged tissue to heal
✓ Supports long-lasting relief

You are in control of your health and your healthcare choices.
